How it works

The TikTok flow, step by step. The app interface is currently in Spanish; the names in quotes below are what you will see on screen.

1. Connect your TikTok account

  1. Open Sistema › Conexiones (System › Connections) and find the TikTok card.
  2. Press Conectar (Connect). Your browser opens TikTok's official authorization page.
  3. Log in on TikTok if needed and review the permissions: user.info.basic (show the connected account), video.upload (send a draft to your inbox) and video.publish (post directly).
  4. Authorize. TikTok returns you to the app, which shows Conectado (Connected). The login happens only on TikTok's site.

2. Schedule a video

  1. Open the video's package and press Programar publicación… (Schedule post).
  2. Choose the date and time and tick TikTok (and any other network).
  3. Review the caption. Each network has its own text.

3. Choose draft or direct post

Draft: choose Enviar a mi bandeja (send to my inbox). At the scheduled time the video appears in your TikTok inbox and you publish it from your phone.

Direct post: choose Publicar directamente a la hora (post directly at the time). The app shows your TikTok account, then you set:

  • who can view the video (no default; you must choose);
  • whether to allow comments, duets and stitches (off by default);
  • whether the video promotes a brand, product or service.

By posting you accept TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation. Press Programar.

4. Publish and check

  1. At the scheduled time the app uploads the video to TikTok. Before that you can move or cancel it in the Calendario (Calendar).
  2. The app asks TikTok for the upload status and shows it on the post. A direct post can take a few minutes to appear on your profile.

5. Disconnect

Press Olvidar (Forget) on the TikTok card to remove the credentials from your PC. You can also revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services permissions.
