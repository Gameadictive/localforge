Plan short videos and publish them to your own accounts, on time.

LocalForge Publisher is a Windows desktop app made by Casi Humanos. You prepare a short vertical video and its caption, choose a date and time, and the app sends it to your own social accounts through each platform's official login and API.

See how it works Features

What it does

  • Calendar. Plan videos on a weekly or monthly calendar.
  • Schedule. Pick a date, a time and one or more networks. One caption per network.
  • Connect your own accounts with each platform's official login. Disconnect at any time.
  • Publish. At the chosen time the app uploads the video. Nothing is posted unless you scheduled it.

TikTok

LocalForge uses Login Kit so you can connect your own TikTok account, and the Content Posting API to send a scheduled video to that account as a draft in your TikTok inbox. Direct posting with the caption, privacy setting and interaction options you choose will be available once TikTok has audited the app. Details, step by step, on How it works.

Your data stays on your computer

Videos, captions and schedules are stored locally. Access tokens are kept in the Windows Credential Manager of your own PC. LocalForge has no servers and does not sell or share data. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
