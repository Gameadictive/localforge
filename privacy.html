Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

LocalForge Publisher ("the app") is a Windows desktop application made by Casi Humanos ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

1. Where data is stored

The app runs on your own computer. Scripts, videos, schedules and settings are stored only in the app's data folder on that computer. We do not operate servers for the app and we do not receive copies of your content or data.

2. Data from connected platforms

When you connect an account (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or LinkedIn) through the platform's official login, the app receives an access token and basic account information (such as your account ID and display name) with the permissions you approve. The app uses this only to show which account is connected and to upload the videos you schedule, with the caption and settings you choose.

For TikTok, the app uses the scopes user.info.basic (to show the connected account), video.upload (to send a video to your TikTok inbox as a draft) and video.publish (to post a video you scheduled to your TikTok account).

3. How tokens are protected

Access tokens are stored in the Windows Credential Manager of your computer. They are never written to logs and never sent anywhere except to the platform that issued them.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data. Content leaves your computer only when the app uploads a video you scheduled to the platform you chose. The app does not include analytics, advertising or tracking.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect an account in the app at any time and revoke access in each platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services permissions). Deleting the app's data folder removes all locally stored data.

6. Children

The app is not intended for people under 18.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
