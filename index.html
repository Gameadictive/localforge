Create, schedule and publish short videos from your desktop.

LocalForge Publisher is a Windows desktop app made by Casi Humanos. It helps a creator write, produce and schedule short vertical videos, and publishes them to the creator's own social accounts at the chosen time.

How it works

  1. Create. Draft a short script, review it and produce a vertical video (voice, images, subtitles and licensed music) on your own computer.
  2. Plan. Put each video on a weekly or monthly calendar and write the caption for every network.
  3. Connect. Sign in to your own accounts (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn) with each platform's official login. You can disconnect at any time.
  4. Publish. At the scheduled time LocalForge uploads the video with its caption through each platform's official API. Nothing is posted unless you scheduled it.

TikTok

LocalForge uses Login Kit so you can connect your TikTok account, and the Content Posting API to send a scheduled video to your TikTok account, either as a draft in your TikTok inbox or as a post with the caption and privacy setting you chose in LocalForge.

Your data stays on your computer

Videos, scripts and schedules are stored locally. Access tokens are kept in the Windows Credential Manager of your own PC. LocalForge has no servers and does not sell or share data. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
