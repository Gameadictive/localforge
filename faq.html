FAQ

Who is LocalForge Publisher for?

For creators who want to plan short vertical videos and publish them on their own accounts at a set time. It is a Windows desktop app made by Casi Humanos.

Does the app post without my approval?

No. Scheduling a post is your approval. You can move or cancel it in the calendar until it is sent.

Do you see my TikTok password?

No. You log in on TikTok's own page in your browser and approve the permissions there.

What TikTok permissions does the app ask for, and why?

user.info.basic to show which account is connected; video.upload to send a draft to your inbox; video.publish to post a video you scheduled.

What is the difference between draft and direct post?

A draft arrives in your TikTok inbox and you finish it in the TikTok app. A direct post is published by the app with the privacy and interaction options you chose.

Why can a direct post only be "Only me"?

TikTok limits direct posting by apps that have not passed its audit to private posts, and the posting account must be private. Direct posting will be available once the app is audited.

Where are my videos, captions and tokens stored?

On your computer. Tokens are in the Windows Credential Manager. We run no servers and do not receive your content. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I disconnect or delete my data?

Press Forget on the account card, revoke access in the platform's settings, and delete the app's data folder to remove everything stored locally.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows.
